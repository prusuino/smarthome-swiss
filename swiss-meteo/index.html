Swiss Meteo for Home Assistant

MeteoSwiss weather forecast, rain radar and warnings

The MeteoSwiss local forecast for your place (nine days, hour by hour, with the uncertainty range), the live values of the nearest SwissMetNet station, the official warnings matched to your point, pollen counts with the MeteoSwiss load classes and the station's history back to 1980 and the precipitation radar as a loop around your place on the swisstopo map, with a motion-based estimate for the next hour — all from open data, read sparingly. Optional and off by default: air quality, UV index and a pollen forecast from Open-Meteo, labelled as modelled values, not official MeteoSwiss data. Triggers and conditions for the automation editor, heating-degree-day and solar figures, and five dashboard cards in three colour schemes.

Highlights

  • Everything official, in one device: the MeteoSwiss local forecast, the live values of a SwissMetNet station, the official warnings and the pollen counts — all from open data, read by one integration.
  • Reads sparingly: a forecast parameter costs about 40 KB instead of the 33 MB file it lives in, thanks to range requests; the hourly forecast is a few megabytes a day, and a traffic sensor shows it.
  • Built for the automation editor: triggers (rain expected, rain cleared, frost tonight, new run, warning issued, warning cleared), conditions (rain expected, dry, frost expected, warning in force) and ready-made blueprints — no templates needed.
  • Figures for heating and solar: heating degree days, daily mean temperature, global radiation and sunshine sums for today and tomorrow, as sensors.
  • Rain and frost at a glance: the next rain as a timestamp, "rain expected" for the coming hours, "frost tonight" for the garden.
  • Official warnings matched to your place: the MeteoSwiss warnings (via MeteoAlarm) are tested against your forecast point itself, with a banner on the cards.
  • Pollen with the MeteoSwiss load classes: seven species from the nearest of the 15 pollen stations.
  • Rain from a gauge nearby: besides its weather stations, MeteoSwiss runs about 140 automatic rain gauges at other places. Where one of them is closer to you, pick it and the measured precipitation comes from it, everything else still from the weather station.
  • Optional: air quality, UV index and pollen forecast: MeteoSwiss publishes none of the three as open data for a point, so a switch (off by default) adds them from Open-Meteo — modelled CAMS values on a device of their own, clearly labelled as not official MeteoSwiss data.
  • Decades of station history: one action imports the station's archive (hourly since 1980, daily since the station started) into the long-term statistics.
  • Five dashboard cards: hourly chart with uncertainty bands, nine-day strip, daily list, map of Switzerland, sky card — in the Swiss Meteo palette, your Home Assistant theme or a glass look, in sizes from small to XL, with animated or still icons, all with a visual editor.
  • Set up in a minute: from your Home Assistant location or by typing a place or postal code; German, English, French and Italian; diagnostics download with your location redacted.

How to install

Open Settings → Marketplace in Home Assistant, search for Swiss Meteo and download it. Restart Home Assistant, then add the integration under Settings → Devices & services. Free and open source, no account needed. The full documentation is in the README on GitHub.

Français: Prévisions MétéoSuisse, radar de pluie et alertes

La prévision locale de MétéoSuisse pour votre lieu (neuf jours, heure par heure, avec la plage d'incertitude), les valeurs en direct de la station SwissMetNet la plus proche, les alertes officielles appliquées à votre point, les pollens avec les classes de charge de MétéoSuisse, le radar de précipitations en boucle autour de votre lieu sur la carte swisstopo (avec une estimation d'après le déplacement pour l'heure à venir) et l'historique de la station depuis 1980 — le tout en open data, lu avec parcimonie. En option, désactivé par défaut : qualité de l'air, indice UV et prévision pollinique d'Open-Meteo, signalés comme valeurs de modèle et non comme données officielles de MétéoSuisse. Déclencheurs et conditions pour l'éditeur d'automatisations, degrés-jours de chauffage et valeurs solaires, et cinq cartes de tableau de bord en trois palettes.

Données : Office fédéral de météorologie et de climatologie MétéoSuisse (open data, CC BY 4.0) ; alertes via MeteoAlarm (EUMETNET) ; en option qualité de l'air, indice UV et prévision pollinique : Open-Meteo (données de modèle CAMS, CC BY 4.0)

Italiano: Previsioni MeteoSvizzera, radar delle precipitazioni e allerte
