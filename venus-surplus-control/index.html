Marstek Venus battery control from PV surplus

Marstek-Venus-Batterie mit PV-Überschuss steuern

Piloter une batterie Marstek Venus avec le surplus solaire

Gestire una batteria Marstek Venus con il surplus solare

Runs a Marstek Venus battery from your PV surplus, next to the battery of your inverter or alone: it charges with what the first battery leaves, covers house demand while the first battery delivers, and keeps the two from charging each other. Every decision comes with its reason, limits are learned instead of entered, and a watchdog restores the RS485 control mode — no YAML, starts in dry run.

Steuert eine Marstek-Venus-Batterie aus dem PV-Überschuss, neben der Batterie deines Wechselrichters oder allein: Sie lädt mit dem, was die erste Batterie übrig lässt, deckt den Hausbedarf, während die erste Batterie liefert, und verhindert, dass sich die beiden gegenseitig laden. Jede Entscheidung kommt mit ihrem Grund, Grenzen werden gelernt statt eingetragen, und ein Watchdog stellt den RS485-Steuermodus wieder her — kein YAML, Start im Probelauf.

Pilote une batterie Marstek Venus à partir du surplus photovoltaïque, à côté de la batterie de votre onduleur ou seule : elle se charge avec ce que la première batterie laisse, couvre la consommation de la maison pendant que la première batterie fournit, et empêche les deux de se charger l'une l'autre. Chaque décision est accompagnée de sa raison, les limites sont apprises au lieu d'être saisies, et un watchdog rétablit le mode de commande RS485 — sans YAML, démarrage en mode test.

Gestisce una batteria Marstek Venus con il surplus fotovoltaico, accanto alla batteria del vostro inverter o da sola: si carica con ciò che la prima batteria lascia, copre il fabbisogno della casa mentre la prima batteria eroga e impedisce che le due si carichino a vicenda. Ogni decisione ha il suo motivo, i limiti vengono appresi invece che inseriti e un watchdog ripristina la modalità di controllo RS485 — senza YAML, avvio in modalità di prova.

Highlights

Das kann die Integration

Points forts

Punti di forza

Diese Liste gibt es nur auf Englisch.

Cette liste n'existe qu'en anglais.

Questo elenco è disponibile solo in inglese.

  • Charge from real surplus only – AC charging follows the surplus in 100 W steps, less a buffer you set, capped so that AC charge plus the Venus' own PV stay below the device limit. While your first battery (BYD, Pylontech, …) is still absorbing the surplus, the Venus waits. When the first battery stands at its own charge limit, the Venus takes what it leaves; the limit is recognised by itself, without a setting. On a good solar day ("Venus first") it may start early in parallel: when today's forecast is above a threshold you set, or when the remaining forecast or a projection of the current surplus shows that both will fill up.
  • Discharge against house demand only – from 100 W up to your discharge limit, with hysteresis, a reserve and an export guard that follow the demand: in 50 W steps a step below the demand while a first battery covers the rest, in 10 W steps close to the demand when the Venus is the only source, so that a house drawing 200 W at night is served as well as one drawing 2 kW. With a first battery, only while it is already delivering, empty, or delivering nothing at all. "Empty" can follow the reserve you set on your inverter.
  • Standby otherwise – and the two batteries do not charge each other: while the Venus charges, a first battery that starts to discharge a little lowers the charge command and a larger discharge stops the charge; while the Venus discharges, it stops as soon as the first battery charges.
  • RS485 watchdog – the Venus firmware drops the RS485 control mode and resets its power registers on restarts, app or cloud access. The watchdog restores the control mode and the last command: every five minutes, and at once when the control mode drops, when the Venus comes back after a restart or a lost connection, or when the control mode is found off as the integration starts (switched off in the app in the meantime).
  • Discharge cap – a ceiling you set from the dashboard that every command respects and that is enforced again when the app or a firmware reset raises the register.
  • Modbus self-heal – reloads the Marstek Venus Modbus integration after a long connection loss.
  • Safe on bad data – missing readings, a lost Modbus link or a surplus sensor that has fallen silent put the Venus into standby instead of letting it act on old values; a repair notice says which.
  • Dry run – new entries start in dry run: the controller decides and writes every command it would send to the logbook, but touches nothing. Switch it off when the decisions look right.
  • Manual buttons – charge, discharge, discharge down to a target SoC, stop and back to auto.
  • Diagnostics – target mode, reason and the decision in words, every value behind it as attributes, a Control quality sensor that says when the Venus does less than commanded and why, a diagnostics download, and a logbook line for every command with its reasons.

How to install

Installation

Installation

Installazione

Click Add to Marketplace to add the repository to the Home Assistant Marketplace (formerly HACS), download it and restart Home Assistant. Then add the integration under Settings → Devices & services. Free and open source, no account needed. The full documentation is in the README on GitHub.

Klicke auf Zum Marketplace hinzufügen, um das Repository zum Home Assistant Marketplace (früher HACS) hinzuzufügen, lade es herunter und starte Home Assistant neu. Füge die Integration danach unter Einstellungen → Geräte & Dienste hinzu. Kostenlos und quelloffen, kein Konto nötig. Die ganze Dokumentation steht im README auf GitHub (englisch).

Cliquez sur Ajouter au Marketplace pour ajouter le dépôt au Marketplace de Home Assistant (anciennement HACS), téléchargez-le et redémarrez Home Assistant. Ajoutez ensuite l'intégration sous Paramètres → Appareils et services. Gratuit et open source, sans compte. La documentation complète se trouve dans le README sur GitHub (en anglais).

Fai clic su Aggiungi al Marketplace per aggiungere il repository al Marketplace di Home Assistant (ex HACS), scaricalo e riavvia Home Assistant. Aggiungi poi l'integrazione in Impostazioni → Dispositivi e servizi. Gratuito e open source, nessun account necessario. La documentazione completa è nel README su GitHub (in inglese).
